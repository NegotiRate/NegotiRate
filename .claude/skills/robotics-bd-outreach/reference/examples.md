# Examples

Illustrative only. Targets and details are invented to show shape and variance. Adapt to the real recipient, and never reuse an opener across two live messages. Note how each one opens differently: that variance is the point.

## Anti-example: the Bliq message (what got called "AI slop")

Do not reproduce this shape. It scored 94% AI and drew an angry reply.

What it did wrong:
- Drafted straight off the advert, so it recited their own stack back at them (perception, sensor fusion, localisation, prediction, planning and control) — every one of those was lifted from the JD they wrote.
- Told them, in effect, that the role was hard to fill and the talent rare.
- Uniform sentence rhythm, promotional adjectives, the milestone-then-sympathise-then-pivot arc.
- Led with relevance to Pete rather than a real observation about Bliq.

The fix is not to soften those lines. It is to throw out the arc: open on one real thing about Bliq that was not in the advert, be plainly a specialist, name one fitting client, offer whole-stack coverage, and close on their plans.
